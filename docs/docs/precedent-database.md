# Precedent Database

This document tracks relevant precedents associated with:
- pedestrian shade systems,
- transit shade systems,
- temporary cooling infrastructure,
- overhead streetscape systems,
- signal-mounted infrastructure,
- and climate adaptation interventions.

The purpose is not to identify exact replicas of the concept, but to identify:
- analogous infrastructure,
- operational lessons,
- governance models,
- maintenance realities,
- and public reception.

# Important Boundary

A precedent does not automatically imply:
- regulatory approval,
- structural equivalence,
- or transferability to signalized intersections.

Each precedent must be interpreted within its own operational and governance context.
