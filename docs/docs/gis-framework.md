# GIS Screening Framework

This document outlines potential GIS layers and screening methodologies relevant to evaluating candidate intersections for pedestrian heat mitigation interventions.

---

# Candidate GIS Layers

## Pedestrian Activity
- pedestrian counts,
- crossing volumes,
- transit ridership,
- wait-time exposure.

## Transportation Infrastructure
- signalized intersections,
- transit stops,
- sidewalk widths,
- curb ramps,
- median geometry,
- lane count.

## Heat and Climate
- land surface temperature,
- thermal hotspot mapping,
- tree canopy coverage,
- solar exposure,
- urban heat island intensity.

## Safety
- pedestrian crash history,
- conflict zones,
- roadway speed,
- turning movement conflicts.

## Equity and Vulnerability
- socioeconomic vulnerability,
- elderly population density,
- disability indicators,
- transit dependency,
- environmental justice indicators.

---

# Preliminary Screening Logic

Potential screening criteria may include intersections with:
- high pedestrian exposure,
- low existing shade coverage,
- elevated thermal conditions,
- significant transit activity,
- and sufficient geometric clearance.

---

# Potential Data Sources

- City GIS portals,
- TxDOT datasets,
- transit agency datasets,
- NOAA heat datasets,
- EPA EJScreen,
- OpenStreetMap,
- satellite imagery,
- local sidewalk inventories.

---

# Important Boundary

GIS screening alone cannot determine feasibility.

Field verification, engineering analysis, accessibility review, utility review, and operational review would still be required before considering implementation pathways.
