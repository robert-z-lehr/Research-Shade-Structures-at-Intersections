# Research Proposal

## Working Title

Decision Analytics for Pedestrian Heat-Mitigation Infrastructure at Urban Intersections.

## Core Research Question

How should cities evaluate and prioritize pedestrian heat-mitigation interventions at urban intersections under engineering, regulatory, lifecycle-cost, equity, maintenance, safety, and public-acceptance constraints?

## Motivation

Many urban heat interventions are evaluated within disciplinary silos. Thermal studies focus on MRT, UTCI, PET, WBGT, air temperature, or surface temperature. Transportation studies focus on safety and operations. Asset-management studies focus on lifecycle cost. Municipal projects often focus on implementation feasibility. Few frameworks integrate all of these dimensions into a reproducible decision-support method.

## Proposed Contribution

Develop and validate a modular decision-analytic framework consisting of:

1. Intervention taxonomy
2. Feasibility filter
3. GIS screening
4. Thermal-performance assessment
5. Lifecycle cost analysis
6. Multi-criteria decision analysis
7. Validation and sensitivity analysis

## Role of SMS

Stoplight-mounted shade structures are treated as one candidate intervention rather than a predetermined solution. The framework should remain useful regardless of whether SMS is ultimately feasible at specific sites.

## Initial Deliverables
