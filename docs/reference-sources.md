# Reference Sources

## Federal Guidance

### FHWA — Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD)

Primary U.S. guidance governing traffic control devices, signal visibility, placement, operational consistency, and related roadway communication systems.

Official source:
https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/

---

### FHWA — Signalized Intersections Informational Guide

Reference for signalized intersection operations, geometry, pedestrian considerations, and safety.

Official source:
https://safety.fhwa.dot.gov/intersection/conventional/signalized/

---

### U.S. Access Board — PROWAG

Guidance for accessibility within public rights-of-way.

Relevant topics include:
- sidewalk clear width,
- curb ramps,
- pedestrian access routes,
- detectable warnings,
- protruding objects.

Official source:
https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/

---

### FHWA — Pedestrian and Bicycle Safety Resources

Federal pedestrian safety resources and crossing guidance.

Official source:
https://highways.dot.gov/safety/pedestrian-bicyclist

---

## Engineering Standards

### ASCE 7

Widely used U.S. structural loading standard.

Relevant topics include:
- wind loading,
- uplift,
- component loading,
- risk categories,
- local exposure conditions.

Official source:
https://www.asce.org/publications-and-news/asce-7

---

### AASHTO Guidance

Potentially relevant:
- Green Book,
- Roadside Design Guide,
- LRFD specifications,
- pedestrian and bicycle facility guidance.

Official source:
https://www.transportation.org/

---

### MASH (Manual for Assessing Safety Hardware)

Crashworthiness evaluation framework for roadside hardware.

Official FHWA context:
https://safety.fhwa.dot.gov/roadway_dept/countermeasures/reduce_crash_severity/

---

## Important Boundary

This repository should distinguish between:

1. peer-reviewed findings,
2. municipal policy,
3. engineering standards,
4. conceptual hypotheses,
5. and unvalidated assumptions.

A concept may be thermally useful while still being operationally infeasible, structurally infeasible, financially infeasible, or noncompliant with roadway standards.
