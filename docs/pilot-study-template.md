## Research questions
1. Does the intervention reduce MRT, UTCI, or time-weighted pedestrian exposure at the actual waiting area?
2. Does shade change where pedestrians wait, their route choice, crossing compliance, or crowding?
3. Can the intervention operate without unacceptable accessibility, visibility, structural, drainage, utility, or maintenance conflicts?
4. What is the cost per person-minute of protected exposure?

- MRT, UTCI, air temperature, humidity, wind, and solar radiation
